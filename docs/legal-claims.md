# Legal claims on the Dinymeo site

This is not legal advice. It records how v1 treats statements from the client brief.

## Kept, with a footnote

| Brief wording | How it appears | Why |
|---|---|---|
| WHO-GMP, GLP, ISO certified facilities | Shown as **partner manufacturing sites**. Certificates belong to those sites and are available to qualified partners on request. | Claiming a certificate the company does not hold is a misleading advertisement under the Consumer Protection Act 2019 and UCPMP 2024. |
| Allopathic contract manufacturing | Not the company name or the SEO title. | The corporate deck names the company Dinymeo Lifesciences, a formulation house. PCD franchise, export, and making a partner’s brand are the channels. The phrase “contract manufacturing” is not used in titles, descriptions, or keywords. |
| Pan-India delivery / on-time TAT | Kept as an operational promise, not a guaranteed SLA. | Contracts should still set dates in writing. |
| Therapeutic area list (cardiology, antibiotics, …) | Categories only. No brand names, doses, or indications. | Drugs and Magic Remedies Act 1954; UCPMP 2024 — no public promotion of a specific prescription medicine. |

## Softened or removed

| Brief wording | v1 treatment |
|---|---|
| “Premier pharmaceutical company” | Not used. Puffery that is hard to prove. |
| Invented statistics (employee counts, turnover) | Not used. | Still unpublished. |
| 500+ approved formulations, founded 2018, export regions | Published, with a footnote on the stats band and in the disclaimer. | Taken from the September 2026 corporate deck as the company’s own statement. Not a regulator’s count. |
| Output chart (55 / 70 / 85 / 110 million units) | Not published. | Round projected totals in the deck are not corroborated. |
| Disease indications (hypertension, GERD, and similar) | Not published. | Therapy names and dosage forms only. |
| Doctor testimonials (Lloyd-style) | Not used. Restricted under UCPMP. |
| Consumer CTA to buy medicines | Not used. Site is B2B only. Click-to-call / WhatsApp / email for partners. |

## Pages that must stay

- `/legal/disclaimer` — B2B scope, certification footnote, medicines law.
- `/legal/privacy` — Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (enquiry form).
- `/legal/terms` — IP, quotes are not contracts, Pune jurisdiction.
- Footer quality note on every page that mentions WHO-GMP.

## Logo and type

The live SVG is traced from the supplied **Dinymeo golden ratio Final LOGO** PDF/AI (and the `final/` lockup). It is the company device, not a redrawn lookalike. Using a mismatched mark on a pharma site is a trademark risk; the site now uses the original outlines.

The packaged **Mont Heavy DEMO** font file is still **not** loaded on the website (demo licences forbid that). The wordmark you see is the outline already drawn in the logo artwork.
